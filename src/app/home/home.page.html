About

I specialize in developing custom scientific field applications for conservation organizations, transforming paper-based data collection into comprehensive digital solutions tailored to each reserve's unique needs.

My work focuses on creating robust apps that function reliably in challenging field conditions—from remote island locations with limited connectivity to rugged environments where standard technology fails.

Working as a consultant for organizations like Nature Seychelles, I design and implement end-to-end digital systems for conservation data collection, including turtle monitoring, seabird surveys, invasive species tracking, and habitat assessments.

I also develop citizen science mobile applications that enable public participation in conservation initiatives, expanding the reach and impact of conservation programs. Beyond scientific research, I'm passionate about helping community-centered initiatives where technology can empower local communities to take an active role in environmental stewardship, creating sustainable solutions that benefit both conservation goals and community development.

Each application is built from the ground up to address the specific workflows, data requirements, and operational constraints of individual sites—ensuring that technology serves the science rather than the other way around.

Featured Project Mar 2024 - Present

ARM - App for Reserve Management

Associated with Nature Seychelles | Funded by SeyCCAT's Blue Grants Fund

The Application for Reserve Management (ARM) is a comprehensive digital conservation data collection system developed for Nature Seychelles' Cousin Island Special Reserve. The app replaces paper-based methods with an integrated digital platform for critical conservation activities.

Turtle Nesting Monitoring
Tortoise Monitoring
Seabird Census Surveys
Invasive Species Tracking
Beach Profiling
Illegal activities

The system includes activity monitoring features that track step count, time spent, and various user metrics to help improve field methodology and operational efficiency. Real-time analysis and AI-generated reports provide conservation managers with actionable insights for better decision-making and resource allocation.

Deployed across rugged tablets with offline data collection, GPS integration, and automated synchronization—significantly improving efficiency and data quality for wardens, scientists, and volunteers.

Comprehensive Training Program

We implemented a 4-week intensive training program designed to equip Cousin Island's conservation team with full digital proficiency:

  • Real-world scenario exercises simulating field challenges
  • Collaborative group training sessions
  • Individual competency assessments
Result: 100% certification rate with all staff and volunteers demonstrating confidence and proficiency in digital conservation workflows.

The ARM conservation technology platform has gained significant media attention in the Seychelles, with coverage from leading publications and national television.

Seychelles Nation (November 10, 2025) and Today News (November 5, 2025) both featured comprehensive articles highlighting how the ARM app is revolutionizing conservation data collection at Cousin Island Special Reserve.

Additionally, SBC Television broadcast two news segments—one in English and another in Creole—showcasing the app's impact on field operations and its role in modernizing conservation monitoring practices.

This custom-built field application replaces decades of paper-based monitoring systems, enabling wardens and volunteers to digitally record turtle patrols, seabird censuses, invasive species tracking, beach profiling, and more—all with robust offline capabilities designed specifically for remote island conditions.

Developed in partnership with Nature Seychelles and supported by SeyCCAT (Seychelles Conservation and Climate Adaptation Trust), this project demonstrates how targeted technology solutions can deliver meaningful impact for biodiversity conservation.
